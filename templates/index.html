ABOUT PROJECT

Student Performance Prediction

{{ cfg.SITE_NAME }} is a machine-learning web application that estimates how a {{ cfg.UNIVERSITY }} student will perform in their final assessment. It learns from attendance, coursework marks, study habits and a few background factors that matter in Zimbabwe, such as A-Level points, accommodation, fees funding and internet access.

Lecturers can spot students who may need extra support early, and students can see which habits move their mark the most.

  • Predicts the final mark (0–100%) from attendance, coursework and study habits
  • Compares Linear Regression, Decision Tree and Random Forest
  • Feature selection shows which inputs matter most
  • Evaluated with 5-fold cross-validation: average error, R² and pass/fail accuracy
  • Datasets: Computer Science, Law and Medicine, plus your own CSV

HOW IT WORKS

Our Workflow

From raw student data to a predicted mark in six clear steps.

  1. 1

    Collect data

    200 students per programme with attendance, coursework, study habits and background factors. Add your own CSV any time.

  2. 2

    Prepare data

    Numbers are scaled and categories (e.g. accommodation) are encoded inside a pipeline, so test data never leaks into training.

  3. 3

    Select features

    Permutation importance shuffles one input at a time to measure how much the predictions get worse without it.

  4. 4

    Train three models

    Linear Regression (a straight-line formula), Decision Tree (yes/no rules) and Random Forest (many trees voting), plus a "guess the average" baseline.

  5. 5

    Evaluate fairly

    5-fold cross-validation: every student is predicted by a model that never saw them. We report average error, R² and pass/fail accuracy.

  6. 6

    Predict and advise

    Enter a student profile to get the forecast mark, class and the changes that would help the most.
